Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Prio collects, why it is used, and the choices you have when using the app.

Last updated: 24 July 2026

1. About Prio

Prio is a personal productivity application that helps users organise tasks, projects, routines, university information, reminders, goals and calendar items.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Prio, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address and encrypted authentication credentials.
  • Content you create, including tasks, notes, projects, routines, reminders, calendar events, university subjects, assessments and related progress information.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as browser, device and sign-in information provided by our service providers.
  • Local preferences stored in your browser, such as theme, onboarding status or interface settings.

3. Google Calendar information

When Google Calendar integration is available and you choose to connect it, Prio may request permission to create, update and delete calendar events on your behalf. Prio only uses this access to provide calendar-sync features that you request.

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. You may disconnect it at any time. Prio does not use Google Calendar data for advertising and does not sell it.

Google API data: Prio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain and improve Prio; authenticate accounts; save and display your content; operate requested integrations; prevent abuse; troubleshoot problems; and communicate about support or important service changes.

5. How information is stored

Prio currently uses Supabase for authentication, database hosting and related backend services. Information may also be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers used to deliver the website.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers that help operate Prio, when you request an integration, when legally required, or when necessary to protect users, Prio or others.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep account information and user-created content while your account remains active or as reasonably needed to provide the service. You may delete individual content inside Prio where the feature is available.

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the email below. We may retain limited information where required for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

8. Your choices

  • You may choose what content to add to Prio.
  • You may decline or revoke optional Google Calendar permissions.
  • You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • You may stop using Prio at any time.

9. Children's privacy

Prio is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users must also meet any higher minimum age required in their country.

10. International users

Prio is operated from Australia. Your information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate, subject to their security and privacy practices.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Prio changes. The revised date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect. Material changes may also be communicated within the app.
